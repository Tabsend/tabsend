Settings

Sending

Now: clicking the icon shows your devices so you can pick one.

You get 4 seconds to undo. Right-click the icon to pick a different device.
Keyboard shortcut Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + S does what the Tabsend icon does. A second shortcut, not set at first, always sends in one click to the “Send to” device.

Receiving

History Every tab your devices send each other, and whether it arrived. A tab that never arrived can be sent again from there. The newest 50 stay on this browser for offline use.

Devices

    Add a device Connect another browser or your phone to this group with a link or QR code. Useful when “Send in one click” is on, since the icon then no longer shows your devices.

    Finish setting up Tabsend first, then come back here to add a device.

    Scan with a phone's camera, or open the link in another browser. If Tabsend isn't installed there yet, the link shows how to install it.

    Connection

    Relay …

    Moving to the Tabsend hosted service? Sign up at tabsend.org, then paste your setup link here. This browser keeps using your current relay until the new one accepts it. After that, pair your other devices again.

    Advanced details

    For a home-network relay, use its browser address. With the included self-signed LAN setup, that is http://<LAN-IP>:8786; https://<LAN-IP>:8787 is for the Android app.

    You can paste a relay address or a setup link. Tabsend checks whether it is the relay you are already on (then only the address changes and your devices stay paired) or a different one (then it opens setup, and keeps the current pairing until the new relay accepts this browser).
