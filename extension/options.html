Sending

Clicking the toolbar icon
The chooser lets you pick a device every time (default). Quick-send sends straight to your primary device, with a 4-second “Undo” on the page.
Keyboard shortcut Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + S does what the toolbar icon does. A second, unbound shortcut sends straight to your primary device.

Receiving

History Every tab your devices send each other, and whether it arrived. A tab that never arrived can be sent again from there. The newest 50 stay on this browser for offline use.

    Advanced details

    For a home-network relay, use its browser address. With the included self-signed LAN setup, that is http://<LAN-IP>:8786; https://<LAN-IP>:8787 is for the Android app.

    Tabsend checks whether the address is the relay you are already on (then only the address changes and your devices stay paired) or a different one (then it opens setup, and keeps the current pairing until the new relay accepts this browser).
